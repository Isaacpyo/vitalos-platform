    AI & Data

    • Pipeline: an AI & Data form enquiry becomes an opportunity in New on its own (next action "Review enquiry and reply", due the next working day). Any other enquiry gets one with Create opportunity on the enquiry.
    • Stages move only along allowed steps. Won, Lost and On hold need a reason; Won also needs the signed scope reference, typed by you. Only a super admin reopens a won or lost opportunity.
    • Scorecard: six criteria from 0 to 5, weighted 25/20/20/15/10/10. 80+ is a proposal candidate, 65–79 needs more discovery or a narrower scope, under 65 decline or defer. A risk override blocks it whatever the score.
    • The customer-visible summary may be shared with the customer; internal notes never are.
    • Offers hold proposed prices: commercial assumptions, not published. Budget tracks spend against the £1,000 speculative ceiling; it is not a spending cap. Super admins edit both; service leads can view them.
    • Opportunities closed more than 24 months ago have their contact details removed (aidata_retention).

    Documentation

    • REST API reference (read-only, Bearer tokens from Settings).
    • Records are archived rather than deleted, and every change appears in Activity.
    • Security questions or a suspected incident: contact the primary owner straight away.
