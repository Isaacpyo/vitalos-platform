What happens next

  1. The text is read: contact details and organisation by rules, the rest by AI when it is on.
  2. You see each field with the quoted source beside it. Unknown fields stay blank.
  3. We warn you about possible duplicates, then you create the opportunity or link it.

Safe by design

  • Pasted text is untrusted; it can never give the AI instructions.
  • AI output is a suggestion, labelled, and never saved without your approval.
  • Nothing is emailed or sent to anyone.
