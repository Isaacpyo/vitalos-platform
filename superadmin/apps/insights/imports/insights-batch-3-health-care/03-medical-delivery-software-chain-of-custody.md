A parcel goes missing. In retail, that is a refund. When the parcel is a blood sample or a controlled drug, it is an incident, and someone will ask for the record.

## Samples: the packing is the sender's job

Diagnostic samples usually travel as UN3373, Biological Substance, Category B. By road, they must be packed to ADR packing instruction P650: a primary receptacle, secondary packaging and outer packaging, with either the secondary or the outer layer rigid. The sender is responsible unless a contract hands packing duties to the carrier. Packed and marked correctly, the package isn't subject to the rest of ADR \[1\].

The outside carries the UN3373 diamond, with sides of at least 5 cm, and "Biological Substance, Category B" beside it. It also shows the sender's and recipient's details and a responsible person's contact. Dry ice adds a Class 9 label and UN 1845, and spills, theft or damage need an incident report \[2\].

## Medicines: the record is the pharmacy's duty

The GPhC's guidance on pharmacy services at a distance (February 2025) asks pharmacies to risk-assess how and when medicines are delivered, naming fridge lines and controlled drugs, and to track packages so they reach the right person \[3\]. Records should include the patient's consent to the delivery method, the method, the dispatch date and the record of successful delivery. Pharmacies that use couriers need due diligence, written service-level agreements and ongoing monitoring \[3\].

The guidance is blunt about home delivery: there is "a bigger risk of medicines being lost or delivered to the wrong person" \[3\].

## What good delivery software captures

| Moment | What gets recorded |
| --- | --- |
| Collection | Item type (sample, controlled drug, fridge line), packaging and marking check, first scan |
| Each handoff | Scan, time, person, place |
| In transit | Temperature reading where the item needs one; an alert when time in transit runs long |
| Delivery | Recipient check; signature or photo; for controlled drugs, both patient and driver signatures |
| Exception | Failed delivery, damage or spill, with an incident report |

## Software records; it doesn't pack

No app makes a box leak-proof or keeps a fridge line cold. What it can do is make the right step impossible to skip, and leave a record that answers an auditor in minutes, not days.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is responsible for packing a UN3373 sample?

The sender, such as the clinic, lab or surgery, unless a contract gives packing duties to the carrier \[1\].

### What delivery records should a pharmacy keep?

The patient's consent to the delivery method, the method, the dispatch date and the record of successful delivery \[3\].

### Does a courier app replace temperature-controlled packaging?

No. Packaging keeps the item safe; the app proves each step happened.
