## What shipped

- **A1, A6 hero system.** `HeroArtwork` (`packages/ui/src/patterns/artwork/`): deterministic SVG per page family (home, what-we-do, industries, about, labs, insights, careers, trust, contact, not-found, legal) with per-item variants. Dark hero for top-level and family landing pages; new light compact hero for offers, articles, roles, legal pages and Trust topics, with the header solid on those routes (`heroToneForPath`). No hero photograph anywhere; the hero's largest paint is now its headline.
- **A2, A3 icons and diagrams.** `LineDiagram` for the six solutions (AI Transformation = the AI journey staircase); lifecycle ring discs carry line icons; new icons `filter`, `ruler`, `release`, `cycle`, `stages`, `dataflow`; `chip` no longer used for AI Transformation or AI & Data.
- **A4 industries.** Industry panels and heroes draw the Industries family artwork, one variant per industry; no people photographs.
- **A5 MediaSlot.** Production renders nothing and closes up (`mediaSlotRenders`); `reserve` keeps a frame; previews and dev unchanged. Every slot has a `source`; `about.founder` and `insights.author` pre-registered for WS2 and WS3. `docs/review-2026-10-17/IMAGE-BRIEF.md` generated by `pnpm media:slots`.
- **A7 offer hero.** Engagement panel (duration, who it is for, what you receive, next phase) from the offer's fields.
- **A8 Turnstile.** `theme` from the surrounding tone scope.
- **A9 motion.** Reveal, RevealItem, the solution panel, the practice label and the ring centre move only, never clip. Reduced motion: unchanged global rule plus Motion's `reducedMotion="user"`; the homepage artwork's entrance is `motion-safe` only; the aurora and glass loops were already behind `no-preference` and the pause switch.
