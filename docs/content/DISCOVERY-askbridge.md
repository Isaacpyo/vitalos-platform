## Unverified, partly verified, or cut

- **"Operated" by Vitalos.** The site says "A product of Vitalos Technologies Limited"; operation is the owner's statement, as for the other products.
- **Prices, plans, allowances and the free trial.** On the site, but the product template allows no figures.
- **Customer results.** The site's businesses (Harbour Lettings, Sam) are fictional and labelled "Illustrative scenario"; nothing on the page names or implies a customer.
- **Viewing requests and calendars.** The site says Askbridge "does not connect to your calendar or book viewings by itself". Not claimed.
- **Data location and redaction.** On the site's FAQ with caveats (the model runs outside the UK; pattern matching "cannot catch everything"). Left to Askbridge's own Security page rather than restated here.
- **Service businesses and product questions tabs.** Only the lettings tab is rendered without JavaScript; the other two tabs are cited by label only, and the tiles say nothing beyond the label and rows 4 and 12.
- **Launch state.** On 22 Oct 2026 askbridge.vitalos.co.uk was served with `noindex, nofollow`, its legal pages were marked drafts, and billing was in test mode. Linking to it from vitalos.co.uk was the owner's request.
