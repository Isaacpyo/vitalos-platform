# Discovery: Products and Fauward (17 Oct 2026)

- **Navigation source:** `packages/cms/src/navigation.ts` (structure) and `locked-copy.ts` (labels), mapped to `@vitalos/ui` props in `apps/web/src/components/site-chrome/nav-model.ts`. The header mega-menus, the mobile sheet and the footer all render from that model, so a column added there appears everywhere. `llms.txt` and the sitemap do not read the nav: they have their own builders (`features/seo/llms-txt.ts`, `features/seo/sitemap.ts`).
- **Header capacity:** three menus (ADR-031). Live What We Do already has Practices, Solutions and Labs plus a card. A fourth column needs about 68rem against about 59rem at 1024px, so it would overflow. Who We Are has Company and Industries plus a card, which leaves room for one column.
- **Footer:** five cells are live (What We Do, Explore, Company, Trust, Podcast) in `xl:grid-cols-5`. Explore lists the section indexes.
- **About:** `app/(site)/about/page.tsx` → `templates/about/AboutPage.tsx`. The copy is repo-file content (ADR-028): the `about` route page in `packages/cms/src/draft/content/pages.ts`. Its first four headed paragraphs render as one 2×2 card grid (`bodyBands`). A paragraph with a link used to make its section "rich", which split it out of the grid. Cards now keep inline links (`BodySection.inlineLinks`, `FeatureGrid` `richText`).
- **External links:** no shared ExternalLink component. Rich text adds `rel="noopener noreferrer"` to https links, `ResourceLinks` uses an `arrow-up-right` icon, and the footer podcast links use `rel="noopener"`. Nav links had no external support. None is needed now, because the menu links to `/products/fauward`.
- **Analytics:** there is a closed taxonomy (`features/analytics/events.ts`) and one delegated click listener (`components/analytics/site-analytics.tsx`). No `outbound_click` or `cta_click` existed; `outbound_click` was added. Custom events are dropped by Vercel on the Hobby plan (DECISIONS 23).
- **JSON-LD:** the full Organization is on the homepage (`organizationJsonLd`), and commercial pages emit one graph via `pageGraph`. `validateJsonLd` allows a fixed list of types and requires on-origin URLs. `SoftwareApplication` was added to the allowed types, with its `url` as the one off-origin exception, and it must be https.
- **Images:** `MediaSlot` + `content/media-slots.ts` (`docs/design/IMAGE-SLOTS.md`). There was no `public/media` yet, so no slot rendered a file before this change.
- **fauward.com:** `https://fauward.com` answers 307 → `https://www.fauward.com/` 200.
