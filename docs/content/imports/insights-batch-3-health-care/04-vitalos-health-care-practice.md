Vitalos is launching a health and care practice for private clinics, GP practices, NHS teams and the companies that serve them.

It starts with four fixed-scope engagements: the Clinic Admin Audit, Systems Integration, NHS Assurance Readiness and Medical Logistics Digitisation. Each ends with something working, not a slide deck.

Two products are moving into pilot. Admin agents for clinics handle inbox and referral triage, letter drafting and coding support, with a person reviewing every output. Fauward, the logistics platform Vitalos built and runs, is being extended for specimen couriers and pharmacy delivery.
