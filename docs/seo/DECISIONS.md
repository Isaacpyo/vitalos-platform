| 14  | **IndexNow**: the key is `INDEXNOW_KEY` (public by design), served at `/indexnow-key.txt` on the canonical production host only and sent as `keyLocation`. | 15  | **Performance**: the hero photograph (the LCP element on every page) gets a 640 w derivative and a lighter 960 w (both re-encoded from the 1672 w master), and a `sizes` hint of half the viewport below lg. Under a black overlay of 72% or more, the difference cannot be seen. Geist Mono, which sets only the small eyebrows, is no longer preloaded. Lab budgets (`tooling/seo/budgets.json`) guard against regressions: performance ≥ 85, simulated LCP ≤ 4.0 s, TBT ≤ 300 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1. | Simulated LCP fell from 3.8–5.0 s to 3.0–3.8 s, and with applied slow-4G throttling LCP is about 2.1 s (it was 2.96 s), inside the 2.5 s target. On a local server, simulation counts every early request against LCP. The remaining weight is the Motion runtime (ADR-024, owner-approved). The 2.5 s target is a p75 field target, measured by Speed Insights. |
