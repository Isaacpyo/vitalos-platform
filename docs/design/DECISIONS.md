# Pages redesign: decisions log

Decisions taken while carrying the homepage design across the site (`feat/pages-redesign`, from 15 Oct 2026), where the brief was unclear or met the repo as it is. Newest last.

| #   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Branched from `feat/web-hero-refresh` (the homepage redesign, not yet merged), so the PR to `main` also carries the homepage commits.                                                                                                                                             | The brief says to branch from the branch that holds the homepage redesign.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 2   | **No dark theme.** Pages use the existing tone scopes (`light`, `muted`, `dark`), and screenshots are light only.                                                                                                                                                                 | The design tokens have no `prefers-color-scheme` or `data-theme` theme, and the brief forbids changing the tokens. A site-wide dark theme would be a token change and needs an ADR and owner approval.                                                                        |
| 3   | **Five practice pages**, not six.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | There are five locked practices (`practiceNames`). Labs is separate (owner direction, 15 Oct 2026).                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 4   | Copy lives in the repo (`packages/cms/src/draft/content`, ADR-028), not Sanity. Edits are logged in `COPY-CHANGES.md`.                                                                                                                                                            | Sanity was removed (ADR-028).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 5   | Inner-page heroes reuse the approved homepage photograph under a heavier black overlay, static (no drift or glints).                                                                                                                                                              | It keeps every hero in the same visual family without a new asset. A static backdrop needs no pause switch (WCAG 2.2.2); the looping motion stays on the homepage only.                                                                                                       |
| 6   | The header is clear over every page's dark hero (`overlay` on every route), not only the homepage.                                                                                                                                                                                | Every page now starts with the dark hero, and the header's structure is unchanged.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 7   | Practice, solution and industry detail heroes use the approved illustrations and photographs already on the homepage. `MediaSlot` is only used where no approved image exists.                                                                                                    | Approved assets beat placeholders, and there is no duplicate file.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 8   | `MediaSlot` ids are typed (`MediaSlotId`), so an unregistered id fails `typecheck` in CI. A unit test also checks that `docs/design/IMAGE-SLOTS.md` matches the registry.                                                                                                         | It meets "a check fails CI if a slot is used but not registered" with the existing gates and no new CI job.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 9   | `MediaSlot` labels show when `NEXT_PUBLIC_MEDIA_LABELS=1`, and by default in development and on Vercel previews (`VERCEL_ENV=preview`). They never show in production.                                                                                                            | That matches the brief, and previews show labels without a new Vercel setting.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 10  | Screenshots are JPEG (quality 70), one page per template at 1440, 768 and 390 (for example one practice page stands for all five), plus one contact sheet per family.                                                                                                             | Every page at three widths would add hundreds of megabytes to a public repo. Pages of one template share their layout.                                                                                                                                                        |
| 11  | Visual-regression baselines (`tests/visual`, non-gating, `linux-ci`) are recorded by CI. All of them were removed, including the homepage's, so the CI `visual` job records a complete new set (`visual-baselines-for-adoption`) for the owner to adopt (tests/visual/README.md). | Baselines are Linux-only and cannot be produced on Windows. The old homepage baseline predated the homepage redesign, which is in the same PR. The Phase 1 pixel diff shows the extraction left the homepage unchanged. The workflow records only when no CI baselines exist. |
| 12  | Structural section headings that the tests and the IA rely on ("Problems", "Capabilities", "Typical journey", "Sector realities", "Common questions", etc.) are kept. Eyebrows and layout change around them.                                                                     | The brief changes presentation, not structure.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 13  | The closing band on inner pages is `TerminalCTA` `feature` with the locked closing heading, and the page's own primary action where it has one (for example an offer's "Discuss this offer").                                                                                     | It is the homepage closing pattern; "reuse the homepage CTA labels".                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 14  | Proof sections (work, testimonials, stats, logos) keep rendering only from real content; nothing is added. Work, Insights, Careers and People are gated or empty, so only their presentation changes.                                                                             | Integrity rule.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 15  | `MediaSlot` files are served pre-sized without the image optimiser (`next/image` `unoptimized`), like `CmsImage`.                                                                                                                                                                 | That keeps the site convention, and Vercel image optimisation counts against a usage quota.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 16  | Card grids keep their columns for a single item, and hero media is capped at 15rem below md.                                                                                                                                                                                      | A lone related card should not stretch full width, and on a 390px screen the hero keeps its title, lead and action on the first screen.                                                                                                                                       |
| 17  | The Labs index keeps its filterable asset list; category pages use the homepage Labs bench.                                                                                                                                                                                       | The maturity filter and its counts are tested behaviour on the index. The bench would repeat every asset link there.                                                                                                                                                          |
| 18  | On How We Work the lifecycle ring shows titles and discs only; each stage's description follows once, in the anchored stage list.                                                                                                                                                 | Otherwise the same description is read twice, by sight and by assistive technology.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 19  | Role pages end with their own apply action, not the client closing card.                                                                                                                                                                                                          | "Have a problem worth solving?" speaks to clients, not candidates.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 20  | The Trust Centre closing card's action is the controlled evidence request, with Talk to Vitalos as the secondary action.                                                                                                                                                          | The overview's one call to action stays the evidence route.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 21  | The contact page heading is restored to "Bring us the problem."                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | It had inherited the homepage's new closing heading through a shared reference. The locked copy says the contact page keeps it.                                                                                                                                               |
| 22  | Gated templates (Work, Insights, Careers, People) were checked against the e2e fixtures, not screenshotted.                                                                                                                                                                       | In live content they 404 behind their gates, so there is nothing to capture on the dev server.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 23  | The error page (`app/error.tsx`) uses a dark band that is not pulled under a header.                                                                                                                                                                                              | That boundary renders without the site shell. `global-error.tsx` stays unstyled by design.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 24  | Raw colour literals introduced by the homepage redesign were replaced with token `color-mix()` values. The owner-directed black overlays are marked `raw-colour-ok`.                                                                                                              | This meets the Stage 9 rule; `pnpm check:raw-colours` passes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
