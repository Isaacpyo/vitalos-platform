Vitalos is a UK technology consulting and engineering firm. The identity says one thing: strategy and engineering converge into an operating outcome. Every rule below serves that idea.

Be bold in scale and restrained in palette. Few elements, each one big: a huge statement, one large Convergence, one confident colour field. Never fill space with small decoration. Start from `BrandBoard` to see the whole direction at once.

## Positioning and character

- Present Vitalos as a multi-practice firm, never as an AI agency or a web agency. Consulting and engineering get equal weight on every first screen.
- Write and design for the journey: understand the problem, define the opportunity, architect the solution, engineer the system, deploy it, operate and improve it.
- A client can start with consulting, discovery and assessment, or go straight to engineering when requirements are clear. For complex, uncertain or high-risk work, say that discovery comes first.
- Aim for credible, intelligent, precise, calm, modern, engineering-led and enterprise-ready. Premium without pretension; forward-looking without AI hype.
- Never look like a web-design or marketing agency, a cheap outsourcer, a crypto or cyberpunk startup, a healthcare company, a generic SaaS template, an IT-support shop or a dated corporate consultancy.
- Never use robot heads, glowing brains, circuit-board heads, neural-network graphics, glowing spheres, AI spark icons, purple gradients or sci-fi interfaces.

## Logo

- Use the files in the Logos asset group; never redraw, recolour, stretch or re-letter them. The wordmark's notched A is part of the mark.
- Use `vitalos-horizontal` as the default lock-up on white and off-white, `vitalos-primary-stacked` where the space is square, and `vitalos-symbol` alone where the name is already present (favicons, avatars, app icons, footers).
- On midnight use `vitalos-horizontal-on-midnight` (colour) or `vitalos-horizontal-reversed` (white). For one-colour print or stamps use `vitalos-horizontal-mono`. `vitalos-symbol-outline` is for watermarks and large decorative use only.
- Keep clear space of half the symbol's height on every side. Minimum width: 96px (25mm) for the horizontal lock-up, 16px for the symbol.
- The symbol is two bars that converge: the larger cobalt bar is strategy, the smaller mineral bar is engineering, and the point they meet is the outcome. Reuse that reading in every diagram: cobalt for strategy and primary paths, mineral for engineering and the outcome.
- The supplied artwork is raster PNG. Request vector SVG masters (colour, one-colour, reversed) before print production or favicons.

## Colour

- Build every layout as dark navy plus neutral surfaces: roughly 60% `midnight` or `surface-100`, 25% `off-white` and `white`, 10% `cobalt`, 5% `mineral`. Spend colour in large fields, never as scattered accents.
- Use a full `cobalt` signal field once per document or campaign: a chapter opener, a cover edition, one social tile. On it, text is `on-cobalt` at 18px bold or larger, and the Convergence's upper band turns `midnight`.
- Use `midnight` for major backgrounds, headlines and high-authority surfaces; `off-white` as the main light surface and `white` for raised panels and report pages.
- Use `cobalt` for the primary accent, a single hero word, data marks and the strategy path. Use `cobalt-600` for filled buttons with `on-action` text, and `accent-text` for links.
- Use `mineral` sparingly: engineering paths, the outcome node, one positive status. Pair a status colour with a word.
- Never set body text in `cobalt` or `mineral` fills; use `cobalt-600`/`cobalt-300` and `mineral-700`/`mineral-300`.
- Do not use gradients, glows or transparency. Use flat colour and hairlines.
- Text meets 4.5:1 in both themes (7:1 for `ink`, `ink-muted`). Notes on every token name the grounds they were checked on.

## Typography

- Set display, headlines and major statements in Manrope (`--font-display`): weight 800 for `display-xl` and `display-lg`, 700 for `headline` and `title`, tight negative tracking as tokenised.
- Set body, interfaces, tables and reports in Inter (`--font-sans`) with `body` (16/24) as default and `body-lg` for lead paragraphs.
- Make headlines huge and few: one `display-hero`, `display-xl` or `display-lg` per page, left aligned, tight tracking, at most 12 words, ending in a full stop set in `cobalt`. The cobalt full stop is a signature; use it on every major statement.
- Use `eyebrow` (uppercase, tracked) for numbered stage labels such as `01 ASSESS`; use tabular figures for numbers in tables.
- Set the annotation layer in JetBrains Mono (`--font-mono`): `mono-label` uppercase for figure numbers, rulers, stage codes and document references; `mono-data` for hex values, references and contact details. Never set sentences in mono.
- All three families load from Google Fonts: Manrope 500/700/800, Inter 400/500/600, JetBrains Mono 400/500.

## Layout

- Build on the 12-column grid: container `layout-container`, gutter `layout-gutter`, section padding `space-24` on desktop. Align everything to column edges.
- Use generous whitespace, oversized editorial headlines, thin `stroke-hairline` rules in `line`, and modular sections separated by rules rather than boxes.
- Keep corners square (`radius-none`); use `radius-sm` on buttons and inputs only. Do not stack rounded cards. Do not lay every section out as a dashboard.
- Use diagrams that look drawn by an architect: orthogonal lines, one diagonal angle (`path-angle`, 60 degrees, taken from the symbol), circular nodes.

## Graphic language

- The signature device is **the Convergence**: two bands, strategy in `cobalt` above and engineering in `mineral` below, rising and falling at `band-angle` (30°) and merging into one horizontal path that leaves the frame. It is the symbol turned to face forward. See `GraphicMotif`.
- Use it big and cropped. It enters from an edge or starts as a clean vertical cut on a column line, merges, and leaves through the right edge. Never show it whole and small, centred, rotated, outlined, with a third band or with a gradient.
- Keep a gap between the bands of `band-gap` (about 6% of the band thickness, at least 4px). On a cobalt field the upper band becomes `midnight`.
- Keep copy off the bands except short `mono-label` captions (white on cobalt, midnight on mineral).
- Add the annotation layer for engineering precision: a 12-step mono ruler, column hairlines in `line-dark`, corner crop marks, figure numbers ("FIG. 01"), and dimension marks (θ 30°, t, g). Use it lightly: one or two elements per layout.
- For diagrams, use nodes and paths: hollow nodes are pending, filled nodes are decided, the `mineral` node is the outcome. Paths are `stroke-path` weight, horizontal or at 60°/30° only.
- Show AI progress as a rising staircase (`AIJourney`): fields grow in height and brightness from `midnight-800` to `mineral`.

## Imagery

- Prefer documentary, editorial photography: engineering environments, technical teams at work, infrastructure, data centres, system and product details, architecture, real implementation sites, sophisticated computational imagery.
- Crop tightly, use natural light, keep colour neutral or cool, and let `midnight` overlays sit flat at the edge, never as a glow.
- Never use handshakes, people pointing at laptops, generic corporate teams, holograms or stock-style AI visuals.

## Voice

- Write short, confident, plain statements in British English. Say what is delivered and for whom; avoid hype words, exclamation marks and emoji.
- Use the lines in Messaging as headlines. See the Engagement model for the journeys and practice names.
