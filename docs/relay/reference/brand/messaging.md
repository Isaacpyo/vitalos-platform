# Messaging

Use these lines as headlines, section openers and social copy. Keep each one on its own line, with a full stop.

## Core lines

- Strategy that gets engineered. (Homepage hero. The master line.)
- From opportunity to operation. (Journey and sign-off line.)
- Engineering clarity from complexity.
- Technology built for the real world.
- Assess. Architect. Engineer. Operate. (Four-beat process line.)
- AI without the theatre. (AI practice and social.)
- Systems built to survive production.
- Strategy is only useful when it can be executed.

## The positioning sentence

We understand the business problem, architect the right solution, engineer it properly, and can continue operating it.

## Homepage hero copy

- Headline: Strategy that gets engineered.
- Supporting copy: Vitalos helps organisations assess opportunities, design the right systems, engineer production-ready solutions and operate them with confidence.
- Primary call to action: Start a conversation.
- Secondary call to action: Explore our capabilities.

## Rules

- Lead with the outcome the client gets, then the method.
- Say "discovery" and "assessment" plainly. For complex, uncertain or high-risk work, state that discovery comes before engineering.
- Never write "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "unleash", "supercharge" or "magic". Never write AI as a noun with a capital-letter promise ("AI-powered everything").
- Describe AI work in terms of the journey: assessment, prototype, production, managed service.
- Use the same verbs in the same order: assess, architect, engineer, operate.
