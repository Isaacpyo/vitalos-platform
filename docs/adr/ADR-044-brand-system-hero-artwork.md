# ADR 044 Brand-system hero artwork and page families

Status: Accepted (engineering), 17 Oct 2026, review run WS1. Amends ADR-031 decision 12 (every hero dark, on the hero photograph), the imagery parts of ADR-023 (owner comp) and ADR-024 decision 4 (text entrances that clip). Implements run decision 4 (`docs/review-2026-10-17/DECISIONS.md`): off-brand imagery is replaced with brand-system artwork, never generated photographs or stock.

## Context

The 17 Oct review (`docs/review-2026-10-17/FINDINGS.md`, A1 to A9) found that one AI-generated cityscape photograph was the hero background of every page, that the lifecycle ring, the solutions panel and the practice stage used glossy AI-generated 3D illustrations (AI Transformation as a glowing brain on a chip), that Industries used AI-generated photographs of people, and that every hero was the same dark block, so practice, solution, offer and industry pages looked alike. The brand book forbids AI-generated and stock-style imagery, glowing brains, chips, gradients and glows, and gives a graphic language instead: the 12-column grid as hairlines, the Convergence (cobalt strategy band above, mineral engineering band below, at 30 degrees, merging into one path that leaves the frame), nodes and paths at 30 and 60 degrees, and a light annotation layer. Empty `MediaSlot` frames also showed in production (A5).

## Decision

1. **Artwork in code, not images.** `HeroArtwork` (`packages/ui/src/patterns/artwork/`) draws a deterministic SVG from the graphic language with design tokens only (Tailwind token classes; no raw colours, gradients, glows, transparency or text). It is server-rendered, a few kilobytes, decorative (`aria-hidden`) and identical on server and client (a seeded generator, FNV-1a plus mulberry32, no run-time randomness).
2. **Page families.** One composition per family, so a visitor can tell where they are at a glance: `home` (the Convergence at full scale), `what-we-do` (practices, solutions, offers, Work: a smaller Convergence with a decision path of nodes), `industries` (a site plan of orthogonal blocks over a thin Convergence), `about` (About, How We Work: the nine stages as a rising path), `labs` (a specimen matrix with one trial path), `insights` (ruled lines beside one cobalt field; also the podcast page), `careers` (the rising staircase of fields), `trust` (nested frames around one verified node), `contact` (Contact, FAQ, thank-you: two lines meeting at one node), `not-found` (the Convergence cut short) and `legal` (the grid and one rule). Items get their own variant from their slug: each practice, solution, offer, industry, lab type and asset, article and role draws differently within its family. The mapping lives in `apps/web/src/lib/view-mapping/hero-artwork.ts` (families), `practice-artwork.ts` and `industry-artwork.ts` (by locked name). Labs, as a practice entry, draws the Labs family.
3. **Two hero tones.** Top-level pages, and the dark landing pages of the What We Do and Industries families, keep the dark hero under the clear header, as the homepage does; the artwork fills the right of the band from lg (the copy keeps to the left half) and runs as a short strip under the copy below lg. Reading pages (an offer, an article, a role, a legal page, a Trust Centre topic) take a new light compact hero (`PageHero` default tone): off-white, under the solid header, closed by a hairline, the artwork on the right from md and none below md. `heroToneForPath` names the reading pages once; the header uses it to choose `overlay`, so the server HTML already pairs a clear header with a dark hero and a solid header with a light one.
4. **Line icons and diagrams only.** The lifecycle ring shows each stage's line icon (`Icon`, 24 grid, 1.5 stroke, square caps; new icons `filter`, `ruler`, `release`, `cycle`, `stages`, `dataflow`). The solutions panel and solution heroes show `LineDiagram`s in the same language; AI Transformation is the locked AI journey as an abstract staircase of four fields and nodes (assess, prototype, production, managed), never a brain, a chip or a robot. The AI Transformation and AI & Data icons stop using `chip`.
5. **No raster imagery in the site's chrome.** Every AI-generated raster under `apps/web/public/images/` is deleted (`docs/review-2026-10-17/ws1/NOTES.md` lists them); nothing imports `apps/web/app/assets/`. The brand logos (`public/brand/`) and the typographic Open Graph card stay. Real photography enters only through `MediaSlot`s with a written brief (`docs/review-2026-10-17/IMAGE-BRIEF.md`), from a named, rights-cleared source.
6. **`MediaSlot` in production.** A slot whose file is missing renders nothing in production and takes no space; `mediaSlotRenders` lets a hero drop its media column, so the copy and artwork close up. A slot registered with `reserve` keeps its frame. Development and previews keep the labelled placeholder (`NEXT_PUBLIC_MEDIA_LABELS`, `VERCEL_ENV=preview`), unchanged.
7. **Motion never clips text.** Reveal, RevealItem and the showcase panels move content into place and never clip or fade it (amends ADR-024 decision 4, which allowed a clip wipe: a heading caught mid-entrance showed half cut, A9). The homepage artwork rises in once on load behind `motion-safe`; every other drawing is static. Reduced motion keeps the existing global rule (no transitions, no animation).
8. **Turnstile follows the site's tone.** The site has no colour-scheme switch (docs/design/DECISIONS.md 2); its surfaces are tone scopes. Turnstile's default `auto` followed the operating system and drew a dark widget in the light form (A8). The widget now takes the tone of the band it sits in: `dark` only inside a dark tone scope, otherwise `light`.
9. **The offer hero is an engagement panel.** The icon-in-a-card is replaced by a panel of the offer's own fields: typical duration, who it is for, what you receive (first three outputs) and how it connects to the next phase. Empty fields are left out; the summary band below no longer repeats them.

## Options considered

- **Commission photography now:** not possible tonight and needs rights-cleared sources and owner approval. The slots and brief prepare it.
- **Static SVG files in `public/`:** rejected. Code-drawn artwork reads the tone tokens (one drawing serves dark and light), varies per item without a file per item, and passes the raw-colour check.
- **Keep the photograph under a darker overlay:** rejected by run decision 4.
- **Client-side canvas or a generative library:** rejected. It would add JavaScript and a dependency to every page and could not render on the server.

## Consequences

- The largest paint of every hero is now its headline, not a 1672 px photograph; the page loads no hero image at all.
- The homepage hero keeps its copy, glass panel and pause switch; only the background changes (the glints went with the photograph).
- A new family or item variant is a code change in `packages/ui` with a unit test; there is no asset pipeline.
- `apps/web/app/assets/` still holds the owner's original raster files (unused). The owner decides whether to delete them.
- Reading pages under a light hero that 404 show the dark not-found hero under the solid header; acceptable for an error page.
