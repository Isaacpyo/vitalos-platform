# 20 — First-sale readiness

Status: assessed 28 Oct 2026 (launch milestone) · Owner: Business Lead · Supports: VAD-001 to VAD-013; blueprint §23.1

_Can Vitalos AI & Data honestly offer its first paid AI Opportunity Assessment? The blueprint lists ten conditions. Below is the honest status at the time of writing — update the table (with a date) whenever a status changes. "Built" means it exists in the repository or the Super Admin; it is not evidence that a customer has used it._

**Overall: not yet ready to sell.** The internal foundations are built or drafted in this milestone. The remaining gaps are owner actions: legal review of templates, naming the delivery owner, approving the offer and price, and confirming how invoices are raised. No customer, contract or payment exists.
