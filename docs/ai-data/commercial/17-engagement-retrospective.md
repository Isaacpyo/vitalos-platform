Status: draft v0.1, 28 Oct 2026 · Supports: VAD-016, VAD-018 · FDE-10 Learn

_Hold within two weeks of acceptance, closure or a no-go decision — including engagements that did not go well; those teach the most. Use actual figures from the project ledger ([15](15-cost-tracker.md)). Keep confidential customer documents out of general knowledge stores unless expressly permitted. Store the filled-in retrospective with the engagement in the Super Admin; copy only de-identified lessons into templates here._
