# Vitalos AI & Data decision log

Decisions taken for the first launch milestone. Blueprint decisions (DEC-001 to DEC-015) are the strategic context; the entries below record how they were applied here. Status: **Adopted** (taken under the launch brief of 28 Oct 2026), **Assumption** (reasonable inference, revisit when evidence arrives), **Owner** (needs the business owner).

| ID      | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Status     | Revisit when                                                           |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AID-001 | **Build in `vitalos-platform`, not a new repository.** The live site (vitalos.co.uk, Vercel), the enquiry pipeline (Supabase record + durable outbox, Turnstile, rate limits, retention) and the Super Admin (Django, sign-in with TOTP, roles, audit trail, auto-deploy) already exist and fit. A new repository would duplicate them. The blueprint's `vitalos-ai-data/` layout is mapped onto existing folders (ADR-052).                                                                                                                                                                          | Adopted    | A client engagement needs its own isolated deployment                  |
| AID-002 | **Public site at `vitalos.co.uk/ai-data`**, a division section with its own header ("VITALOS · AI & DATA") inside the existing Next.js app. No new domain or subdomain: DNS changes need the owner, and the existing domain is authorised. The existing `/what-we-do/ai-data` practice page stays and links to it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Adopted    | Owner wants a subdomain (e.g. `ai.vitalos.co.uk`): DNS + Vercel domain |
| AID-003 | **Internal pipeline in the Super Admin, not a bespoke CRM or a paid CRM.** No CRM subscription exists; the Super Admin already holds enquiries. An `Opportunity` record (stages New → Reviewing → Discovery → Qualified → Assessment/Proposal → Won/Lost/On Hold) links to the enquiry it came from.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Adopted    | Pipeline volume or sales team outgrows it                              |
| AID-004 | **Enquiry routing without a database migration.** The AI & Data form validates its own fields, then submits through the existing enquiry contract (intent `ai`, source path `/ai-data/contact`). Sector, current process, desired outcome and indicative budget are composed into the stored enquiry text with labels. The notification email gains `X-Vitalos-Pipeline: ai-data` and `X-Vitalos-Offer`; the Super Admin ingest creates an opportunity from it (idempotent per enquiry).                                                                                                              | Adopted    | Structured reporting on these fields is needed (add columns then)      |
| AID-005 | **Budget band on the AI & Data form only**, inside the composed enquiry text, optional, with "Prefer not to say". The main contact form's budget field stays off (stage-1 owner gate unchanged).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Adopted    | Owner approves bands for the main form                                 |
| AID-006 | **Prices are not published on the website.** The blueprint prices (£350, from £1,500, from £3,000, from £250/month) are seeded in the Super Admin as _proposed_ offer prices, editable there. The site says fees are fixed and agreed in writing before work starts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Adopted    | Owner decides to publish prices                                        |
| AID-007 | **Initial segment: UK bookkeeping and accounting practices (about 2–15 staff)** and outsourced finance teams. Evidence: blueprint ranks DocumentOps highest (82/100), its worked ROI example is a bookkeeping practice, the buyer is usually the owner with budget authority, the data (statements, ledgers) is bounded and well-structured, and the work is repetitive and measurable. Uncertainty: the space is crowded (Dext, AutoEntry, Hubdoc), so the wedge is statement-to-ledger reconciliation and exceptions, not invoice capture. No customer interview has validated this yet (VAD-011).  | Assumption | After 10 discovery conversations (VAD-011)                             |
| AID-008 | **Demonstrator: supplier statement reconciliation with exception review.** Extract statement lines (deterministic parser first, model only for layouts the parser cannot read), reconcile against a purchase-ledger export with deterministic Decimal arithmetic, flag missing invoices, amount mismatches, duplicates and unmatched items, route exceptions and uncertainty to a reviewer. Chosen over enquiry classification because enquiry/inbox automation overlaps Vitalos Workforce (keep the businesses distinct) and reconciliation shows the AI/deterministic split the blueprint requires. | Adopted    | Discovery shows a different workflow is funded first                   |
| AID-009 | **Python AI core inside the Super Admin, not a separate FastAPI service on Cloud Run.** The Super Admin is already an authenticated, deployed Python application with PostgreSQL and an audit trail. A separate service would add a deployment, cost and an auth boundary with no current need. `apps/aicore/core/` imports nothing from Django so it can be mounted in FastAPI for a client engagement.                                                                                                                                                                                              | Adopted    | First client deployment needs isolation (separate service/project)     |
| AID-010 | **No Google Cloud resources in this milestone.** The session had no valid Google Cloud credential, and creating a project with billing is a paid commitment needing the owner. Existing hosting is used: Vercel (site), Azure Container Apps (Super Admin, inside its free grant), Supabase (website records). Private document storage uses the Super Admin's existing private object storage when needed; the demonstrator stores only small synthetic text in PostgreSQL.                                                                                                                          | Adopted    | A client engagement or the owner chooses GCP                           |
| AID-011 | **One provider adapter: OpenAI-compatible chat completions** (works with OpenAI, DeepSeek, Gemini's OpenAI-compatible endpoint and others) behind a `ModelProvider` interface, plus a replay provider for recorded responses and simulated test doubles for failure cases. Provider choice per engagement follows data terms, region and evaluation results.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Adopted    | A provider needs a native SDK feature                                  |
| AID-012 | **No live model key is configured for AI & Data yet.** Copying the website's Relay key into this environment was refused by the session's credential policy, and other products' keys (Askbridge, client projects) are not ours to use. The demonstrator runs end to end with the deterministic parser; the model path is off until `AI_DATA_LLM_API_KEY` is set in the Super Admin's production environment. Offline evaluation uses labelled simulated responses; it is **not** evidence that a live provider works.                                                                                | Owner      | Owner adds the key and runs the live evaluation workflow               |
| AID-013 | **Spending controls.** Speculative ceiling £1,000 (blueprint DEC-011), tracked in the Super Admin budget page by blueprint category. Known new spend in this milestone: £0. Model calls are capped per run, per day and per month in the application (a cloud budget alert is not a cap) and the whole demonstrator has a kill switch.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Adopted    | Any paid commitment (needs owner approval)                             |
| AID-014 | **Legal and commercial templates are drafts marked "for qualified review".** No template is presented as an executed or legally approved document.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Adopted    | After legal review                                                     |
| AID-015 | **Public repository hygiene.** The repository is public: commercial templates here are generic; live pipeline records, costs and customer material live only in the Super Admin database.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Adopted    |                                                                        |
| AID-016 | **Branch.** Work lands on `claude/amazing-cray-miqs1x` (the session's designated branch) rather than `feature/vitalos-ai-data-foundation`; same purpose, squash-merged to `main` after checks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Adopted    |                                                                        |
