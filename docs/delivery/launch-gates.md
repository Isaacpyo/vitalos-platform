# Launch gates

- G0 Source: approved remote, deployment source and ownership confirmed.
- G1 Design: Figma system and representative responsive templates approved.
- G2 Content: accurate owned reviewed content; no placeholders.
- G3 Proof: permissions and evidence verified.
- G4 Functional: navigation, forms, redirects and failure paths pass.
- G5 Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA target, keyboard/zoom/reflow audited.
- G6 Performance: agreed budgets and representative CWV tests pass.
- G7 SEO: canonicals, metadata, schema, sitemap and redirect inventory pass.
- G8 Security: threat controls, dependency/secret/CodeQL gates pass.
- G9 Trust/Legal: authorized claims, policies and evidence handling pass.
- G10 Rehearsal: cutover and rollback rehearsed with named owners.

No date bypasses a gate; reduce scope instead.
