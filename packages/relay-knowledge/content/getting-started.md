There are two ways to start. Clients can start with consulting or come directly for engineering.

- Consulting-led: when the problem is known but the solution is not, discovery and assessment create the evidence and roadmap.
- Engineering-led: when the objective, requirements and constraints are clear, we confirm the approach and build.

Discovery is used when it reduces real uncertainty or risk, not by default. To take the first step, use "Talk to Vitalos" on the website or ask Relay for a person, and describe the problem rather than the technology.
