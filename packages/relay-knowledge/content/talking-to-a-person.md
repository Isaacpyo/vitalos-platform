To start a conversation with the team, use "Talk to Vitalos" on the website or ask Relay for a person. You can also email support@vitalos.co.uk.

## How Relay hands over

Relay is the Vitalos AI assistant. When you ask for a person, Relay shows a short form for your name and work email. A person from the team picks up the same conversation with its history in front of them. In working hours a person usually joins in minutes; out of hours we reply by email on the next working day.

## Using Relay

Relay is AI and can make mistakes. It answers only from Vitalos's published information and shows its sources. Please do not share sensitive personal data in the chat.
