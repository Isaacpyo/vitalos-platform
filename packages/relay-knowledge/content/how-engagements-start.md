You can start in three ways: with consulting, with a discovery or assessment, or by going straight to engineering when the requirements are already clear.

## When discovery comes first

For complex, uncertain or high-risk work, discovery comes first. Discovery exists to reduce uncertainty before a large commitment: it is the right start when the problem is unclear, several teams or systems are affected, legacy behaviour is poorly understood, or data and AI readiness are unknown.

## How we work through an engagement

We assess, architect, engineer and operate. A recommendation only matters if it can be engineered, released and run safely, so the same team stays connected from advice to production.

## Taking the first step

Use "Talk to Vitalos" on the website, or ask Relay for a person. Describe the problem rather than the technology; a person from the team will suggest the right starting point.
