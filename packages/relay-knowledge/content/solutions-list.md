Vitalos has six solutions. Each one describes a business problem we solve across our practices:

- AI Transformation: turn AI ambition into operational value.
- Business Process Modernisation: redesign the work, then automate and integrate it.
- Digital Product Transformation: discover, design, engineer, launch and improve a digital product.
- Application Modernisation: modernise in controlled stages towards a platform you can keep changing.
- Data & Intelligence Transformation: build the foundation, then the intelligence on top of it.
- Technology Recovery & Reliability: triage a critical platform, establish a known baseline, stabilise and remediate, then operate it.
