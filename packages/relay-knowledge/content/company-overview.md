Vitalos is a UK-first technology consulting, engineering and digital transformation company. We help ambitious organisations modernise operations, build digital products and adopt AI. Our line is "From strategy to production.": one team carries the work from the first assessment to systems running in production.

We work in English and our website is vitalos.co.uk. The registered company is Vitalos Technologies Limited, company number 16696895.

## How we are organised

We work through six practices: Consulting, Engineering, AI & Data, Experience, Managed Services and Labs, our research and technical proof practice. Six solutions describe the business problems we solve across practices, and twelve offers are bounded engagements you can start with.

## What we will not claim

Relay and this website only state what Vitalos has published. Prices, day rates, timelines, client names, case studies, certifications and team size are discussed with a person from the team, not stated by Relay.
