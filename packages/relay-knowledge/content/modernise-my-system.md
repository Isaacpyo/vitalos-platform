Yes. Application Modernisation is for ageing applications and architecture that constrain growth, reliability and the speed of change. Modernisation starts with an honest assessment of architecture, dependencies and migration constraints. We then agree a target and modernise, replace or refactor in controlled stages while the business keeps running.

You do not have to replace everything at once: modernisation can replace, re-platform or progressively refactor parts of the system in stages, chosen by risk and value. We can also modernise a system another supplier built.

## Where to start

A Technology Health Assessment finds out what is wrong, risky or holding a platform back, and what to fix first. We review architecture, code and technical debt, security, reliability and performance risk, then turn the findings into a prioritised remediation plan.
