One firm, six practices: Consulting, Engineering, AI & Data, Experience, Managed Services and Labs.

- Consulting: clarity, transformation choices, roadmaps and architecture before expensive execution.
- Engineering: serious production software, modernisation, platforms, integration and reliability.
- AI & Data: AI and data from opportunity and foundations through production and managed operation.
- Experience: human-centred products, services, commerce and accessible digital experiences.
- Managed Services: reliable operation, support and continuous improvement for systems Vitalos builds or responsibly inherits.
- Labs: prototypes and experiments. Labs is where we prototype new tools, models and patterns, and publish what we learn. Labs work carries a maturity label, so a prototype is never presented as a supported product or as client work.

A client can start with Consulting, a discovery or an assessment, or go straight to Engineering when requirements are clear.
