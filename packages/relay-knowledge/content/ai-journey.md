Our AI journey has four steps: AI Opportunity Assessment, then Prototype, then Production, then Managed AI.

1. AI Opportunity Assessment: find the AI opportunities worth building: viable use cases ranked by value, feasibility, data readiness and risk.
2. Prototype: an AI Prototype Sprint tests the highest-value AI hypothesis quickly, with real workflows and data where appropriate, so you decide on evidence.
3. Production: engineer the chosen use case into systems that run in production.
4. Managed AI: operate and improve the AI system after launch, through Managed Services.

For complex, uncertain or high-risk AI work, the assessment comes first.
